# 👤 Participant Instructions & Privacy Policy Agreement

## Welcome!

Thank you for agreeing to participate in this facial expression recognition research study.  
Your feedback helps us develop better AI systems for marketing and emotion analysis.

---

## 📝 Instructions for Participants

1. **Study Overview**
   - You will see your own camera feed (“mirror mode”) on a screen.
   - The system will automatically capture an image if it detects a distinct emotion.
   - Simply act naturally—there are no specific tasks or prompts.

2. **How It Works**
   - Captures only occur when an emotion is detected with high certainty.
   - Each image is saved with a timestamp and anonymous code, never with your name or identity.

3. **Session Details**
   - Sessions last about 5–10 minutes, but you may stop at any time. Inform the staff if you wish to withdraw.
   - If anything makes you uncomfortable, please let us know immediately.

4. **Support**
   - Contact research staff whenever needed, now or in the future, with questions or requests.
